# Kestrel K2 Survey Drone: User Manual

## Overview

The Kestrel K2 is a quadcopter designed for agricultural and construction surveys. It weighs 1.38 kg with the standard battery and has a maximum take-off weight of 1.9 kg. The airframe is rated IP54, which means it tolerates light rain and dust but must not be flown in heavy rain or submerged.

## Battery

The K2 uses a 6S 5200 mAh smart battery. Under normal survey conditions with no payload, expect around 38 minutes of flight. With the multispectral camera attached, flight time drops to roughly 31 minutes. In temperatures below 5 °C the battery must be pre-heated to at least 15 °C before take-off; the app will refuse to arm the motors otherwise.

A full charge takes 75 minutes with the standard charger and 45 minutes with the fast charger. Batteries should be stored at around 60 percent charge if they will not be used for more than ten days. The battery automatically discharges itself to that level after ten days of inactivity.

## Flight modes

There are three flight modes. Survey mode flies a pre-planned grid and is the only mode in which the camera triggers automatically. Manual mode gives full stick control with obstacle avoidance still active. Sport mode disables obstacle avoidance and raises the top speed from 15 m/s to 21 m/s; it should only be used by experienced pilots in open areas.

## Return to home

Return-to-home (RTH) triggers automatically when the battery drops to 20 percent, when the remote controller signal is lost for more than 3 seconds, or when the pilot presses the RTH button. Before returning, the drone climbs to the RTH altitude, which defaults to 60 metres and can be set anywhere between 20 and 120 metres.

## Error codes

E-101 means the compass needs calibration. Move at least 10 metres away from cars and metal structures and follow the calibration dance shown in the app.

E-204 means a propeller is damaged or unbalanced. Land immediately and replace the propeller before the next flight.

E-317 indicates that the GNSS receiver has fewer than eight satellites locked. Survey mode cannot start until the count is at least eight.

E-450 is a battery cell imbalance. Stop using that battery and contact support; do not attempt to charge it again.

## Maintenance

Propellers should be replaced every 200 flight hours or immediately if any chip or crack is visible. The motors need inspection every 500 flight hours. Firmware updates are installed through the Kestrel Field app and must never be interrupted; keep the battery above 50 percent during an update.

## Warranty

The airframe and motors are covered by a 24-month warranty. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 300 charge cycles, whichever comes first. Crash damage is not covered unless you purchased Kestrel Care, which allows two replacements per year for a fixed fee.
