# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (2025 edition)

## Working hours and location

Our core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 India Standard Time. Outside of those hours people may arrange their day however they like, as long as their team knows when they are reachable. Every employee can work remotely up to three days a week. Teams that want to go fully remote for a quarter need written approval from their department head.

The Pune office is open from 8:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. The Kolkata office closes earlier, at 19:00, because the building management shuts down the air conditioning after that time.

## Leave

Full-time employees get 24 days of paid leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Unused leave can be carried over, but no more than 8 days roll into the next year; anything above that is lost on 31 March. Leave requests longer than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least three weeks in advance.

Sick leave is separate from paid leave. You can take up to 10 days of sick leave per year without a medical certificate. Beyond that, HR will ask for a certificate from a registered doctor.

New parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave regardless of gender. It can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 18 months after the child's birth or adoption.

## Equipment

On joining, every engineer receives a laptop with at least 32 GB of RAM and a monitor allowance of 25,000 rupees. Laptops are refreshed every three years. If your laptop breaks, raise a ticket with the IT desk using the code HW-REPLACE; a loaner machine is usually ready within one working day.

Personal devices may be used for email and chat, but source code must never be stored on a device that is not enrolled in our device management system.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 40,000 rupees. It covers books, online courses, and conference tickets. Travel to conferences is paid separately from the travel budget if your manager approves it. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase, otherwise the reimbursement is rejected.

## Expenses and travel

Domestic travel is booked through the internal portal called Atlas. Flights must be economy class unless the flight is longer than six hours. The daily meal allowance during travel is 1,800 rupees in metro cities and 1,200 rupees elsewhere.

## Security incidents

If you suspect a security incident, such as a lost laptop or a phishing email you clicked, report it immediately in the #sec-incident channel or call the security on-call number. Do not try to investigate on your own. Reporting quickly is never punished; hiding an incident is a disciplinary matter.

## Performance reviews

Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review has a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a conversation with your manager. Promotions are only decided in the October cycle.
